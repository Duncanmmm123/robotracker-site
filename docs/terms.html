The service

RoboTracker (RT) is an independent application that observes supported public Roblox profile changes and can deliver alerts in Discord. These terms apply to the RoboTracker bot and this website. By installing or using RT, you agree to follow the rules below and the applicable rules of Discord and Roblox.

RoboTracker is independently operated and has no affiliation with or endorsement from Roblox or Discord. Availability and visibility can differ by profile, server, location, endpoint, and over time.

Who may install and use RT

You may install RT only in a Discord server where you have permission to manage applications. You must meet the age and eligibility rules that apply to your Discord and Roblox accounts. If you install RT for a community or organization, obtain the necessary permission and explain to other server managers how the tracker channels are configured.

Only add Roblox accounts when you have an appropriate reason and the authority required by applicable rules. If you’re unsure whether monitoring is welcome, get permission first. Pay particular attention to accounts that may belong to children.

Acceptable use

Use RT to follow public activity in a way that respects the privacy and safety of the people involved. You may not use RT to:

  • Harass, stalk, threaten, target, or impersonate someone, or organize harmful activity around someone’s Roblox presence.
  • Attempt to bypass privacy, access, age, or safety controls, or obtain someone’s private activity or credentials.
  • Publish or redistribute tracker data in a way that exposes people to harm or violates applicable rules.
  • Spam alerts or recipients, interfere with RT’s operation, evade usage limits, or attempt unauthorized access to its systems.
  • Use RT data as proof for discipline, safety decisions, identity verification, or another high-stakes decision.

Report abuse or security concerns through a private ticket in RoboTracker Support.

Server permissions and alert controls

The Discord authorization screen lists the permissions RT requests. RT uses permissions such as Manage Channels and Manage Roles for tracker setup and access, and messaging permissions to deliver and manage alerts. Server operators control which accounts are tracked, who can receive alerts, and the ultimate visibility of channels in their server.

Review the tracker channel after setup and when roles or membership change. Use /settings and the /alerts commands to manage delivery, or use /untrack-account to stop a tracker. Removing RT from the server stops its access through that installation. It does not erase every copy of earlier Discord messages.

Alert timing and accuracy

RT checks public API responses on a schedule. The first observation establishes a baseline. Later alerts may be delayed, skipped, or incomplete because of rate limits, server permissions, upstream outages, or information that changes between polls. Game information, named friends, and session duration can be incomplete or estimated.

Use the application as an informational tool. Verify anything important with the relevant platform before acting. RT does not promise uninterrupted operation, complete coverage, or a fixed delivery time.

Privacy and removing a profile

Read the Privacy Policy for information on collection, storage, visibility, and retention. An account holder can ask RT to review removal after verifying control with /privacy remove and /privacy verify. If the automated workflow is unavailable, the support team can help with the next step.

Do not share passwords, Roblox session cookies, Discord tokens, or database credentials when using the bot or contacting support.

Changes, restrictions, and removal

The team may change features or limits as platform access, reliability, or privacy requirements change. We may disable tracking or restrict access when needed to protect users, address misuse, or operate safely. You can stop using RT or remove it from your server at any time.
